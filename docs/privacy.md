# Privacy statement

Turtlehaven is playable offline. It does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, analytics, advertising identifiers, or gameplay telemetry. Saves and settings remain locally on the player's device. Platform storefronts may process purchases and cloud features under their own policies; optional platform adapters must never be required for play. Support messages contain only information the player chooses to send. Contact: support address to be finalized before publication.
