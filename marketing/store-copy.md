# Steam store copy

## Short description
Guide a young sea turtle across a storm-broken archipelago. Rescue wildlife, revive luminous reefs, solve gentle environmental puzzles, and rebuild a sanctuary in a focused single-player adventure.

## Features
- Restore three distinct islands, a living sanctuary hub, and the heart of a supernatural storm.
- Swim, dive, ride currents, cross changing tides, and read the natural world from an isometric perspective.
- Solve reef-light, shell-pattern, rescue, observation, and water-navigation challenges—without mandatory timers.
- Watch clearer water, returning wildlife, richer music, and evolving characters transform each place.
- Play offline with controller or keyboard/mouse; adjust motion, flashing, subtitles, colors, vibration, controls, and assists.
- A complete 3–5 hour premium journey with optional memories and post-game exploration; no microtransactions.

## Content disclosure
Stylized depictions of injured wildlife, environmental storm damage, entanglement, and brief non-graphic peril. No blood, realistic violence, gambling, in-game purchases, chat, user-generated content, or required online interaction.
