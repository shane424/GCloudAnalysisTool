# Audio provenance

The repository currently contains no binary audio and therefore no third-party audio licenses. Runtime audio hooks and separate Music, Ambience, and SFX buses are implemented, but silence is intentional until commissioned masters pass commercial-rights review.

For release, every imported master must list: title, composer/recordist, source session, license or work-for-hire agreement, allowed platforms, edit permission, attribution wording, and proof-of-license location. Do not substitute preview, AI-demo, Creative Commons NonCommercial, or unclear field recordings. Final review owner: audio director and release producer.
